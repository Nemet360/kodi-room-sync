## 4. Privacy — what leaves your home, and what protects it

When you pair two or more devices, the add-on stores a small record in a
third-party storage service on the public internet. That record contains **what
you watched and how far you got** — titles, episode numbers and timestamps.
Viewing history says a lot about a person. Treat it accordingly.

The **pairing code is the only thing that protects that record.** Anyone who
learns your code can read your household's watch history, and can write to it.
Therefore:

- never post your pairing code anywhere public — not in a forum, an issue, a
  screenshot or a support request;
- share it only with people you would let sit on your own sofa;
- if you think it leaked, create a new home and re-pair your devices.

No account, email address or payment detail is collected by this project. The
author operates no server and receives no telemetry. The third-party storage
provider you choose may log IP addresses and request metadata under its own
privacy policy, which you should read.
